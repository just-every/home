905: ### Agent surface
906: 
907: **Specialist: support agent.** It triages and resolves support requests, finds permitted knowledge and business context, carries out permitted repair and keeps customer recovery and follow-up attached to the original request.

909: Inherits BASE-AGENT-001 to BASE-AGENT-013. The company’s policy decides every call.
910: 
911: **How HappyToHelp computes each class.**
912: - **standard:** reversible internal drafts, ticket organization, triage, private knowledge edits and obligations; facts include affected item count.
913: - **external:** a reply, notification, share, published article or destination newly exposes support content outside the business; facts include recipients and known customer/vendor, new counterparty or public exposure. A proactive insight saved or generated for a visitor whose widget will show it is external, with recipient count and known, new or public counterparty facts.
914: - **money:** a Shopify refund, Stripe refund/subscription charge change or changed support-provider spending; facts include amount, currency and merchant/customer.
915: - **commitment:** a customer promise, accepted offer or editorial sign-off others rely on; facts include value and counterparty.
916: - **accounts:** connecting a service creates an outside provider account, number or paid resource; facts include provider and resource.
917: - **gated:** the provider reserves consent, hosted checkout, verification or personal terms to a person, including a provider response requiring that step; facts include provider and step.
918: - **access:** staff, selected knowledge, customer entitlements, devices or integration/agent grants change authority; facts include affected people and whether it widens or narrows.
