123: #### OC-003 — Roles and authority
124: 
125: **Requirement.** Incident changes require an active authorized human session or an explicitly scoped OC-053 service-account capability (RL-2). A machine is attributed as a service account and never gains human-session, member-management or human-review authority. Viewer reads; responder coordinates; admin manages services, policies, members and import. All active members see all workspace incident data, and admin, responder and viewer permissions are separate from incident-commander attribution. Ingest credentials grant no read or human authority. Bearer and signed intake return only `duplicate`, the stored event `id`, the source `eventId`, `service`, `observedAt` and `acceptedAt` (201 accepted; 200 deduplicated). Routing decisions, snapshots and alert identities stay behind authorized read and export doors; scoped OC-053 reads disclose only granted service records, and archive and export authority remains session-protected. The status-only stakeholder role, with no incident, alert, runbook or archive access, is specified and tracked in OC-061.

275:

The injected native SQL failure and expired publication lease legs distinguish rolled-back publication from retained partial effects that require explicit rollback. The same file's saved-response journeys cover unchanged replay, and its native-edit and later-import journeys preserve records under `NATIVE_EDIT_CONFLICT` and `IMPORT_SUPERSEDED`, including revoked-session refusal. In `tests/import-door.mjs`, the PagerDuty account HTTP journey revokes Business import authority inside the committing batch, injects publication failure, and checks unchanged native records; its identical-byte and reviewed catch-up journeys cover unchanged and changed identities.
