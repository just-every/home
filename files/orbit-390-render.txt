Orbit puts a deal in your NOW queue when a customer replies, scores it from real signals, and shows you why. Your company's policy controls agent sending; with approval required, a person reviews the exact email before it is sent. It is free for the whole team, and you can try it beside your current CRM.

What it does
Move a deal into your NOW queue when a customer replies, books a meeting or submits a form
Score each deal from what customers actually did, with older activity counting for less and every signal behind the score shown
Keep contacts, companies and deals, with your own fields, in a pipeline with your own stages
Let a contact form on your site drop each enquiry onto the right contact
Draft follow-ups, with optional AI, that you review and approve one by one, and schedule them with quiet hours
Cancel scheduled follow-ups automatically when the customer replies
Pull in emails and meetings from Gmail, Google Calendar, Outlook, forwarded mail or a calendar link
Import contacts from a CSV, and export or restore a complete archive of your workspace any time
What it promises
Signals, scores and AI text never grant permission to send. Your company's policy decides agent sending; when approval is required, a person approves the exact saved recipients, subject and body, and later changes invalidate it.
A deal's score shows every signal it used, with source, time and weight, beside the reason for the recommendation.
Orbit uses no tracking script, pixel or cookie, and does not fingerprint visitors.
A reply, a booked meeting, an unsubscribe or a permanent bounce cancels that contact's scheduled messages.
Opt-out is on by default for sequence email and off for one-to-one email. Messages set to carry it include one-click unsubscribe; an identified opt-out marks the contact do-not-contact within a minute and cancels scheduled messages.
A draft goes stale when the customer replies or the deal changes, and a stale draft cannot be sent until you have seen what changed.
Capture never writes a draft or sends a message, and a contact created from captured mail is do-not-contact until you clear it.
If the AI is unavailable, records, scoring, search, hand-written drafts and sending still work.
Merging duplicates needs you to choose the surviving record and every conflicting value, and refuses if either record changed after your review.
Deleting a contact removes their email, signals, drafts and message bodies from records, search, exports and later AI or mail requests within five minutes.
